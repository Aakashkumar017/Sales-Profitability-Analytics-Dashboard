# Sales & Profitability Analytics Dashboard

An Excel project for exploring sales performance and profitability across time, markets, products, customers, and sales channels. The workbook combines an interactive dashboard with supporting analysis views and PivotTables.

## Dashboard Preview

![Sales and profitability dashboard](Screenshot%202026-10-29%20234908.png)

The dashboard brings together revenue, gross profit, gross profit margin, units sold, and visual summaries of sales performance.

## Workbook Views

### PivotTable Analysis

![PivotTable analysis](Screenshot%202026-10-30%20000417.png)

Monthly revenue and gross profit summaries, with country and regional breakdowns.

### Sales Data Analyzer

![Sales data analyzer](Screenshot%202026-10-30%20000603.png)

Country-level sales and profit metrics alongside regional and monthly summaries.

## Analysis Areas

- Revenue, gross profit, and gross profit margin
- Monthly sales performance
- Country and regional comparisons
- Product category performance
- Customer type and sales channel analysis
- Units sold

## Open the Workbook

1. Download `Sales Excel project.xlsx` from this repository.
2. Open it in Microsoft Excel.
3. Use the dashboard and analysis sheets to explore the available summaries and visualizations.
